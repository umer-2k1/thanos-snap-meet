Thanos Snap for Meet

Open a Google Meet call. Then:

  1. Snap your fingers → you disintegrate into dust and your mic mutes.
  2. Snap again → you reform and your mic unmutes.
  3. No snap? Click the on-screen pill or press Shift+S.

The purple pill near the bottom of Meet shows your state: LIVE / MUTED · GONE.

Your real mic is cloned — one copy keeps listening for your return snap even while you're muted.
